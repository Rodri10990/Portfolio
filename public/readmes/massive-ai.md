# Massive AI Workouts — Intelligent Training Hub

All-in-one training hub powered by Gemini for automated plans and session-aware progressions.

## Summary

Built with **Next.js**, **Gemini**, **FastAPI**, and **Framer Motion**.

## Highlights

- Session-driven coaching and progression
- Built-in tracker and exercise catalogue
- **100% data-driven progressions**
